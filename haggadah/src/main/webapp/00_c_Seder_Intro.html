The Seder Plate

The Seder Plate requires at least five components - Zeroah, Beitzah, Marror, Charoset, and Karpas; but has no strict requirements for its layout. The diagram below reflects only one of the many traditions.

  • Zeroah - Roasted shankbone
  • Beitzah - Roasted egg
  • Karpas - Vegetable for dipping in saltwater, most use parsley
  • Chazeret - Romaine lettuce or other bitter leafy vegetable (optional)
  • Charoset - Traditional Passover dip made from fruit, nuts and wine
  • Marror - Bitter herbs such as endive or raddichio; many use horseradish
