הַדְלָקַת נֵרוֹת Candle Lighting
As with the Sabbath and all other holidays, one should light at least two candles to usher in the holiday.
Light the candles and recite the following blessings.
On Friday night, the addition for the Sabbath should be included.
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יי, אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו, וְצִוָּנוּ לְהַדְלִיק נֵר שֶׁל (שַׁבָּת וְ) יוֹם־טוֹב. Blessed are You, The Eternal our God, King of the Universe; Who has sanctified us with His commandments and has commanded us to light candles for (the Sabbath and) the holiday.
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יי אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁהֶחֱיָנוּ וְקִיְּמָנוּ וְהִגִּיעָנוּ לַזְּמַן הַזֶה. Blessed are You, The Eternal our God, King of the Universe; Who has granted us life, sustained us, and enabled us to reach this occasion.
Many include their own personal silent prayers as part of this ritual.
